All games and tournament data come from Lichess.org, the free, open-source chess server, via its public API. The live coverage on Lichess Broadcasts is made possible by Lichess, its volunteers and the tournament organisers. Thank you! If you enjoy this site, consider supporting Lichess.
Blunder King is an independent fan project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Lichess.

Games are analysed on our server with Stockfish every couple of hours. Only finished games are included, and each game appears at most once, with its worst move. Engine evaluations can differ slightly from what you see on Lichess; open the game there for a full analysis.
